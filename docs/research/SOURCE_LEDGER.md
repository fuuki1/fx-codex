The current COT feature needs an additional semantic warning. It subtracts net-position/open-interest ratios across different contracts. The USD leg is ICE U.S. Dollar Index futures, while the other configured legs are individual currency futures. Those contracts have different baskets, quote conventions, participant mixes and open-interest denominators. Normalising each by its own open interest does not make the legs symmetric. The result must be named a **cross-contract public positioning proxy**, not USD customer flow, consolidated FX positioning or a directly comparable pair exposure. Stronger PIT provenance changes when and how the proxy may be used; it does not improve that economic meaning or create an information advantage.
